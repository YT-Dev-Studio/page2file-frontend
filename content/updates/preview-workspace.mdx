## What changed

Page2File treats preview as a workspace rather than a loading screen.
Every section has a title, output size, conversion mode and warning state.

Users can reorder, remove, restore, split and merge sections before starting the
final render. Drag and drop has button-based keyboard alternatives.

The real adapter now receives revisioned preview manifests and short-lived
artifact links from the separately deployed backend. Deterministic mock jobs
remain available only for localhost and test workflows.
