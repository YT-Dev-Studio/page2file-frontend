## What changed

Page2File now has dedicated export pages for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok.
Each page explains what its future extension adapter can preserve and where
platform-specific limits apply.

The product direction keeps private chat export in the active browser tab. The
public URL converter is not presented as a way to bypass authentication.

All platform pages include an independence disclaimer and use test media rather
than third-party brand assets.
