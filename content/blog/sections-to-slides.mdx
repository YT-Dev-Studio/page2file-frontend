## Find the page outline

Headings, landmarks and visual grouping reveal a webpage’s information
hierarchy. Those signals can produce a first slide outline without relying on a
language model.

## Fit one idea to each slide

A section may be too large for one 16:9 slide, while several short sections may
belong together. Split and merge controls let the user correct the initial
mapping.

## Preserve what matters

Editable slides should keep supported text, images and safe links. Visual
fallback is appropriate for charts, canvas and complex composition. The preview
must identify those blocks before the deck is downloaded.

Good conversion creates a coherent presentation sequence, not a stack of
arbitrary browser screenshots.
