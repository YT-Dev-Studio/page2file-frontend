## Web layouts are not paper layouts

Responsive pages choose dimensions from a viewport. Printed documents use fixed
paper sizes and page fragmentation rules. Sticky headers, full-height panels
and overflow containers can behave very differently.

## The print stylesheet may be incomplete

Some sites provide excellent print CSS. Others hide useful content, preserve
navigation overlays or never load lazy sections before printing.

## Preview the document structure

A section-aware exporter can stabilize the page, detect meaningful blocks and
show proposed breaks before rendering. It should still respect the site rather
than claiming a perfect editable copy of every browser feature.

Browser Print to PDF remains a good choice for simple pages with intentional
print styles. Use a richer preview workflow when the page structure needs
repair.
