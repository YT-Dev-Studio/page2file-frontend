## Start with the page boundary

Saving a webpage as PDF sounds like one browser command, but the useful result
depends on what you mean by “the page.” A short article, an infinite feed and a
dashboard with hidden panels need different capture boundaries.

Before exporting, decide whether you need the visible viewport, the complete
document, or only the main article. Expand content that is intentionally hidden
behind controls and remove anything you do not have permission to reproduce.

## Choose fidelity or editability

Visual capture is the safer choice when exact typography and layout matter.
Editable conversion is better when the document must remain searchable and easy
to update. Complex canvas, video and animation cannot always remain editable.

## Review before downloading

A useful preview should expose page breaks, missing images, substituted fonts
and removed unsafe links. Do not treat the final file as the first place where
these compromises become visible.

For private or signed-in pages, capture the current browser tab locally instead
of sending credentials or cookies to a URL conversion service.
