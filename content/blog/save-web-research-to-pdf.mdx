For a research article, documentation page, or reference list, use Editable document when searchable text and links matter. Use Accurate copy when the page's visual arrangement, annotation, or chart is the evidence. A useful archive usually needs the page title, date, URL, and enough context to interpret the saved material.

## Preserve the original page, not only the prose

Open the canonical page rather than an embedded preview when possible. Confirm the author, publication or update date, title, and cited references. Expand footnotes or reference panels that must appear in the PDF. If the article is paginated, Page 2 PDF will not follow the next-page link; save each page separately or use the publisher's own download.

Editable document can keep page text selectable and safe links clickable. That makes it suitable for quotation checks and reference follow-up. Accurate copy can preserve a chart, styled table, or annotation exactly as rendered, but its pages are images.

## Record what the PDF cannot prove

A PDF records one available state. It does not prove that the page never changed, that every linked reference was valid, or that hidden content was absent. Dynamic data, comments, personalized recommendations, and subscription state may differ for another reader.

Add your own citation note outside the saved page if it does not show the retrieval date or URL. Do not alter the original page to create a misleading record. If the material is a living technical manual, retain the version or commit identifier when the publisher exposes one.

For research spread across many URLs, Page 2 PDF is a preservation tool, not a crawler. The [capture-versus-scraping guide](/en/blog/webpage-capture-vs-web-scraping) explains when structured collection is the better method.

## Check links and canvas text

Open a sample of the links in the finished PDF. Unsafe schemes are not retained, and inaccessible targets remain inaccessible. If a chart or document viewer draws labels on a canvas, ordinary PDF text may not contain them. Limited regional OCR can help with a small number of canvas or WebGL regions, but it is not whole-document recognition and must be checked for errors.

The [links, forms, and text guide](/en/blog/preserve-webpage-links-forms-text) covers the Editable document controls. For a direct product overview, use [webpage to PDF with links](/en/chrome-extension/webpage-to-pdf-with-links).

## Respect publisher and workplace rules

Saving a page does not grant permission to redistribute it. Follow copyright, subscription, confidentiality, and research-data policies. If the publisher provides a licensed PDF, citation export, or repository version, that may be a better archival record than a browser capture.

Review the final PDF for missing figures, cookie overlays, collapsed footnotes, or navigation that obscures the article. Keep the active tab open until preparation and preview finish.

## Research-page questions

### Does the PDF include the linked materials themselves?

No. It can preserve safe clickable links, not the contents of every destination. Save required linked pages separately.

### Can I make scanned text searchable?

Page 2 PDF only offers bounded regional OCR for eligible canvas-like areas. Use a dedicated OCR workflow for a scanned document or image-heavy archive.

### Should I use Print optimized?

Try it when the publisher has a good print stylesheet. Use As viewed or Accurate copy when print rules remove context you need.
