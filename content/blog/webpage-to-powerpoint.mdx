## A deck needs hierarchy, not just screenshots

A useful presentation has a title, a sequence and one clear idea per slide.
Blind viewport screenshots usually produce cramped slides with inconsistent
boundaries.

## Map sections to 16:9 slides

Page headings and visual groups provide an initial slide outline. Large
sections can be split, while short adjacent sections can be merged in preview.

Visual mode uses section images for maximum fidelity. Editable mode translates
supported headings, paragraphs, images and links into slide objects. Complex
graphics stay as images with an explicit warning.

## Review presentation-specific limits

Web animation, sticky behavior and video cannot become ordinary slide
interactions. Use posters and safe links, and make font substitutions visible
before final render.
