A chat PDF and an account-data export serve different readers. Choose a PDF for one current conversation that must be reviewed as a document. Choose the platform's own export for broader history, portability, compliance work, or machine-readable records.

## Compare scope before format

Page 2 PDF works from the active Chrome tab. AI / Chat builds a portrait transcript of the current supported conversation, with all messages or replies-only and a limit of the latest 2,000 retrievable messages. It does not enumerate an account or follow conversations in a sidebar.

A provider archive can cover many conversations, profile data, settings, or files. It may arrive as ZIP, JSON, HTML, or another platform format and may require an email wait, administrator role, approval, or account verification. That broader scope is the reason to use it, even when it is less convenient to read.

## Use a PDF for review and handoff

A structured PDF is suited to a support record, approved meeting note, research trail, answer packet, or authorized handoff. It can preserve detected roles, code, tables, citations, file references, and supported media while leaving account navigation out.

The preview must still be checked for missing history, reversed roles, expired assets, or platform changes. A PDF is not proof that the original account contained no other messages, and it is not a backup that can be restored into the service.

The [AI chat guide](/en/blog/export-ai-chat-to-pdf) explains supported assistant adapters. The [browser messenger guide](/en/blog/export-browser-messenger-chat-to-pdf) covers WhatsApp Web and Telegram Web.

## Use native export for portability or full history

OpenAI and Anthropic document account-data exports. Telegram Desktop offers chat-history export. Slack and Teams provide controlled organizational routes with role and policy requirements. Perplexity can export a Session directly to several document formats. Use the provider's current first-party instructions and verify your authorization.

Native export is not automatically complete: retention settings, deleted content, plan, account state, and administrative scope can affect it. Keep the provider's metadata and do not convert a machine archive to PDF if doing so would discard evidence needed for compliance or migration.

## Apply privacy and retention rules to both

Page 2 PDF creates the document inside the extension workflow and does not upload page or conversation content to Page 2 File servers. That product boundary does not decide where you store the finished PDF or whom you may share it with. Provider archives can contain much more personal data and need correspondingly careful handling.

Follow participant consent, workplace policy, legal holds, confidentiality duties, and deletion schedules. If a legal meaning or retention term is uncertain, obtain appropriate review instead of relying on a product guide.

The complete active-tab procedure is on the [Page 2 PDF guide](/en/chrome-extension/how-to-use).

## Export-choice questions

### Can the PDF replace a legal or compliance archive?

Not by default. Use the organization's approved preservation process and confirm evidentiary requirements.

### Can I import a provider archive into Page 2 PDF?

No. The extension reads supported content from an active tab and does not import account archives.

### Should I keep both outputs?

Keep both when policy permits and each serves a documented need: the native package for scope and the reviewed PDF for human reading. Record how they relate.
