## Viewport cuts are not document sections

A long webpage is designed for continuous scrolling. Cutting it every fixed
number of pixels can split headings from paragraphs, crop images and leave
single lines at the top of a page.

## Detect meaningful boundaries

Headings, article sections, repeated cards and strong visual separators provide
better candidate breaks. The converter can score these boundaries and then fit
them to the selected paper size.

## Give the user local control

Automatic detection will never understand every design. A preview needs
reorder, split, merge, remove and restore controls. Keyboard-accessible move
buttons are important even when drag and drop is available.

The final render should apply a small ordered list of operations to the preview
revision, not upload the complete page again.
