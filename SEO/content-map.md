## Inclusion boundary

Include URL, HTML or rendered-webpage conversion to PDF/PPTX; visual and
editable output; long/full-page capture; authenticated browser tabs; AI chats;
and browser-accessible messenger conversations. Scraping terms are limited to
the capture-versus-data-extraction comparison page.

Exclude reverse file converters, presentation creation/templates, generic AI
navigation, coding/API scraping, native-only applications and queries without
a direct Page 2 File job to be done.

## Internal-link rule

Each article links to one product route, one hub and one to three closely
related articles. English content links only to `/en/`; Russian translations
use the identical path under `/ru/`. Product pages link back to their most
relevant articles.
