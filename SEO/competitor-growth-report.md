# Page 2 PDF competitor growth patterns

Scope: US-first SEO/ASO research for a Chrome utility. Public user counts establish product scale, not channel attribution, unless the linked evidence explicitly names the acquisition channel.

The canonical evidence table is `SEO/competitor-growth-patterns.csv`. Each record separates the observed pattern, evidence strength, Page 2 PDF applicability, and policy risk.

## Decisions

- Lead with one result: save the full current page or current supported conversation as PDF.
- Put verified sample output before long explanatory copy.
- Build a small hub-and-spoke system around distinct supported intent; do not mass-generate pages.
- Use en-US Store metadata and screenshots that each prove one workflow.
- Pursue replacement outreach to already-ranking tool lists and how-to pages with a relevant sample PDF.
- Publish only attributable results and confirmed customer cases.
- Keep Slack, Instagram, Discord, Messenger, Teams, account-wide export, and pasted-URL conversion out of product promises until supported.
- Treat Chrome Web Store conversion, successful first use, ratings, and uninstall trend as one acquisition-and-retention loop.

## Evidence limits

- TonePerfect and Sig.mobi publish or are reported with audience figures, but those figures do not isolate organic search.
- ViralMaxing case numbers primarily describe its customers' growth.
- Marketplace install counts confirm scale but do not expose traffic-source attribution.
- Yadaphone is the strongest channel-level evidence because the founder explicitly names Reddit, replacement-list outreach, backlinks, and route SEO.
