# Page 2 File editorial quality checklist

Use this checklist for every published route and future English article. Repeat
the factual, language, and link checks independently for Russian copy.

## Search intent and facts

- Answer the query in the opening paragraph without a generic preamble.
- Name the exact product surface: Page 2 File website, Page 2 PDF active-tab
  extension, or a confirmed Web2File GPT.
- Distinguish Accurate copy image pages, Editable document Chromium output,
  and the semantic AI / Chat transcript.
- State whether the workflow accepts an active tab, exact public URL, uploaded
  HTML/PDF, or screenshots; never merge these input contracts.
- Distinguish the current conversation from account-data export or backup.
- Name the relevant limits: source tab stays open, latest 2,000 retrievable
  messages, bounded lazy loading/OCR, unavailable assets, and adapter changes.
- Treat generic AI matching as conditional and Telegram channels as a distinct
  supported variant. Do not imply universal AI or messenger compatibility.
- Verify platform instructions against current first-party documentation.
- Do not invent speed, limits, pricing, availability, test results or platform
  affiliation.

## People-first SEO

- Use one unique title, description, H1, opening answer, proof, limitation set,
  FAQ, and internal-link path for one intent.
- Use the primary phrase naturally; do not list query variants.
- Use descriptive H2/H3 headings and crawlable contextual anchors.
- Link to the relevant product, hub and sibling articles in the same locale.
- Put a real sample or reproducible proof before secondary feature copy.
- Add a specific, localized image alt description.
- Keep every paragraph necessary; there is no target keyword density.
- Make comparisons concrete and state when Chrome Print, native platform export,
  or another Page 2 File surface is the better choice.

## Review evidence

- Judge depth by decisions answered and evidence supplied, not a target word
  count. Delete paragraphs that repeat the result without resolving a new need.
- Record one to three review cycles for every locale and article.
- Record the checked SERP intent, first-party fact sources and review date.
- Keep automated SEO, anti-slop and link checks separate from editorial
  fact-check and usefulness judgment.
- Record extension evidence as code/test/sample/release-check; record changed or
  unavailable live platforms as `pending` rather than softening the wording.
- Do not mark an article publishable until fact, SEO, anti-slop, link and
  rendered reviews all pass.
- After a third failed cycle, keep the article blocked and record the concrete
  blocker instead of weakening the gate.

## Anti-slop

- Remove canned introductions and conclusions that repeat the article.
- Remove vague attribution such as “experts say” or “studies show”.
- Remove fake first-hand experience and unsupported superlatives.
- Remove repeated three-part lists, mechanical transitions and uniform rhythm.
- Remove “not just X, but Y”, excessive em dashes and rhetorical filler.
- Remove AI clichés including “delve”, “ever-evolving landscape”, “robust”,
  “tapestry”, “crucial” and “underscore”.
- Remove excessive Title Case, bold text, list fragments and redundant summary.
- Scan for placeholders, leaked prompt text, broken citations and duplicate
  paragraphs.
- Scan UTF-8 sources for replacement characters and known mojibake sequences;
  do not treat terminal display encoding as source corruption without a byte check.
- Reject `free`, `instant`, `perfect`, `unlimited`, `works with any`, local-only,
  or privacy claims unless the exact release behavior and evidence support them.
- Do not use an AI-detector score as pass/fail evidence.
