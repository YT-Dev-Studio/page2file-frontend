# Page-family concepts

## Home

The current page remains the canonical visual reference. Refactor only where it
improves ownership or fixes the demo flow; preserve the established hierarchy
and anchor destinations.

## PDF and PowerPoint converters

A task-first workbench: short outcome lead, conversion form, explicit format and
mode, pending/progress/result state, recovery guidance, and concise evidence
about output and limitations. PDF and PowerPoint share a shell while keeping
format-specific copy and affordances.

## GPT workflows

An instruction-to-file flow: source prompt/context, what is retained, resulting
format, ordered steps, constraints, and a direct conversion action. Related
workflows are secondary navigation, not the main composition.

## AI-chat export workflows

A privacy-first export explanation: supported conversation content, local or
extension-based processing path, omissions/limitations, output structure, and
the next safe action.

## Extension guide

An instruction-first guide with accessible tabs, numbered installation/export
steps, media or transcript, troubleshooting, and clear completion cues.

## Blog and articles

The blog is an editorial list with featured and chronological hierarchy. An
article uses a readable measure, metadata, semantic sections, and related
reading only when relationships exist in content.

## Updates and changelog

A chronological history with meaningful date/version hierarchy. Avoid marketing
cards and invented status metrics.

## Legal documents

A strict document shell: title, effective date, operator summary, section
navigation, readable clauses, processors, contact, and release-review status
handled in metadata rather than visible draft banners.

## Security

A trust and boundaries page: processing path, controls, retained data,
responsibility boundaries, incident contact, and explicit limitations without
absolute compliance claims.
